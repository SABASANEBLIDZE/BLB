FAQ

Before you book

Everything important is simple: choose your service, choose your branch, and confirm online.

How do I book?

Press Book Now, choose Vake or Saburtalo, then select your service and time in the booking modal.

Can I choose the branch?

Yes. Every booking starts with a branch choice, and branch cards can open the matching location directly.

How long do services take?

Most services take between 20 and 90 minutes. You can see the estimated time on each service card.

How can I change my appointment?

Use the confirmation details from Altegio or contact the studio so we can help update your visit.

Is booking online?

Yes. Online booking is available through Altegio inside this website experience.
